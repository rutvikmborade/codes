MARATHA EMPIRE

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was a great Indian warrior king and the founder of the Maratha Empire. He was born on 19 February 1630 at Shivneri Fort in Maharashtra. His mother's name was Jijabai, and his father's name was Shahaji Bhosale. He was known for his bravery, leadership, military skills, and excellent administration. He established Swarajya, meaning self-rule, and fought against powerful kingdoms to protect his people. He introduced innovative military strategies, including guerrilla warfare, and developed a strong network of forts. In 1674, he was crowned Chhatrapati at Raigad Fort. He respected women, supported religious tolerance, and worked for the welfare of his people. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj remains an inspiration for generations because of his courage, patriotism, and vision of Swarajya.
